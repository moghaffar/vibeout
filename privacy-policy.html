The short version
  • You don't need an account. VibeOut works with an anonymous ID tied to your device.
  • We use your location only to show places near you and to check you're at a place when you drop a vibe.
  • Vibes are shown publicly only as totals and percentages — never with your identity.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

  • Feedback & support messages — anything you send us, including your email address if you include it.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide the core features: nearby places, live vibes, energy scores, trending places and the leaderboard.
  • Award and track gamification points.
  • Detect and prevent spam, fake vibes and other abuse.
  • Analyse usage in aggregate to improve the Service.
  • Provide café partners with aggregated, anonymous insights about their location (for example, total vibes and peak times). Partners never see who submitted a vibe.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. What other people can see

Vibes appear publicly only as aggregated counts, percentages and scores for a place. Your display name and points may appear on the leaderboard. Turn on Ghost mode to hide your profile from leaderboards; your vibes will still count anonymously toward place totals.
