FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. VibeOut creates an anonymous profile on your phone. There's no email or password.

Why does it need my location?
To show places near you and to make sure vibes come from people who are actually there. We don't store your location history.

I can't call a vibe.
Each place has a short cooldown, and there's an hourly limit to stop spam. Make sure location is allowed and that you're near the place.

How do I change my username?
Open the you tab. Usernames are 3–12 English letters or numbers, must be unique and clean, and can be changed up to 5 times a day.

Someone has an offensive name or is bothering me.
Tap them on the ranks board, then report or block. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I hide from the leaderboards?
Turn on ghost mode in the you tab.

How do I delete my data?
you tab → Privacy & safety → delete my account & data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I own a café. How do I get on VibeOut?
Email us, and we'll set you up with a café dashboard showing live analytics for your place.
